# My Favorite Series: Fallout

*A story of haves and have-nots in a world where there is almost nothing left to have.*

## About the Series

**Fallout** is a post-apocalyptic drama series on **Prime Video**, based on Bethesda's video game franchise. It premiered on April 10, 2024, and it is set about two hundred years after a nuclear apocalypse. It follows people from luxury fallout shelters who are forced to return to the wasteland their ancestors left behind. Season 2 followed in December 2025, and the series has been renewed for a third season.

> "War never changes."

## Main Cast

- I like the deep lore, plot twists, and their connection to the video game series.
